Participant C - Interviewee 

Describe the last time you tried to find an audio clip. 
The interviewee could not recall the last time she specifically searched for an audio clip.

What is your preferred method (audio, video, game, etc) of consuming content? Why?
The interviewee prefers consuming video content because she enjoys having visuals to accompany the content.

Tell us about some of the most used audio/music/podcast apps and sites you use. Why do you use them?
The interviewee primarily uses Apple Music and YouTube Music, exclusively for listening to music. She does not listen to podcasts. She mentioned that she uses these applications because they are available to her for free.

What did you do the last time you wanted to share a short audio clip with a friend? Was it a good experience? 
The interviewee used the voice memo feature in iMessage to send an audio message to a friend. She described the experience as positive because she had a lot to say and found speaking more convenient than typing everything out.

Do you have a preferred length for the content you consume? Why?
The interviewee explained that her preferred content length varies depending on how much free time she has. When she has more time available, she is willing to watch longer content. However, she generally finds herself more inclined to consume short-form content through "doomscrolling."


When was the last time you sent an audio / voice clip to a friend? 
What was the contents?
The interviewee described the content as a goofy video but could not recall its specific details. “I think it would be when I was drunk saying stupid stuff” 

Why did you choose to send an audio clip?
The interviewee did not provide a specific reason for choosing to send the clip.

Was it an individual or a group chat?
The interviewee sent the clip to one person. She mentioned that she has never sent this type of content in a group chat.

Do you have any specific types of clips that you prefer?
The interviewee did not specify a preferred type of clip. 
Have you sent voice notes or audio clips when using a standard messaging app like IMessage?
Yes, the interviewee confirmed that she has used iMessage to send voice notes and audio clips.
 
In the past when you have decided not to send an audio clip, why did you not choose to do so? 
The interviewee explained that she has occasionally decided against sending an audio clip because she felt the recording was too long or her thoughts were disorganized/ “where I sound dumb”. In these situations, she felt that the message lacked coherence and chose not to send it.
